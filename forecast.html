NLDC Forecasting Benchmarks

These operational benchmarks are evaluated against historical hourly grid records compiled by PGCB. Lower Mean Absolute Error (MAE) and Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) indicate superior generalizability across sudden seasonal and weather-driven demand swings.

Model Architecture MAE RMSE Status
Hybrid Ensemble 110.2 MW 142.1 MW Best Choice
XGBoost Regressor 128.5 MW 168.0 MW Stable
Random Forest 134.1 MW 175.4 MW Robust
Baseline Persistence 285.9 MW 362.5 MW Historical
💡 Bangladesh Dispatch Advisory: The Bangladesh National Grid experiences a very steep power demand ascent during the evening peak load window (সন্ধ্যা পিক), caused by residential lighting and cooling spikes. While **XGBoost** captures this fast-ramping behavior accurately, **Random Forest** excels at modeling stable daytime baseline loads (ডে পিক). The **Hybrid Ensemble** merges their outputs to provide the NLDC (National Load Dispatch Centre) with the most stable, low-variance load forecasts, shielding predictions from volatile fuel supply anomalies.
